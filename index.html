ℹ️ How it works

  • ❌ Not halal – tagged/named pork, sausage, gelatine, alcohol in the name, or the recipe's nutrition data shows notable alcohol (e.g. wine in the sauce).
  • ⚠️ Doubtful – meat from permissible animals without stated halal slaughter, meat of unspecified animal, meat stock, or traces of alcohol.
  • ✅ Halal-friendly – vegan, vegetarian or fish with no warning signs.
  • Modes: Strict treats every caution (rennet cheese, trace alcohol, non-fish seafood) as doubtful. Relaxed accepts non-certified meat of permissible animals.
